## Added

### REQUIREMENT REQ-protocol-11

An AlgoChat account phrase SHALL be exactly 25 lowercase words from the 2048-word Algorand (BIP-39 English) list, separated by single spaces, with no leading or trailing whitespace. The first 24 words encode the 32-byte entropy with zero padding bits, and word 25 is the checksum word from SHA-512/256 of the entropy. Rust and TypeScript SHALL reject every other input, including 24 or 26+ words, a wrong checksum, nonzero padding, an unknown or uppercase word, and any other whitespace. From the entropy, Rust SHALL derive the same Ed25519 key and Algorand address as algosdk `mnemonicToSecretKey`, and the same X25519 and ML-KEM-768 keys as TypeScript. Rust SHALL validate, restore and create Ed25519 accounts from a phrase; Falcon-1024 accounts are not supported in Rust. Phrase vectors SHALL be generated by TypeScript and algosdk. No test, log or debug output SHALL print a phrase or secret key.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- `ts/conformance/vectors/07-mnemonic-accounts.json` positives match in TypeScript (`verify.mjs`) and Rust (`rs/tests/conformance.rs`), and every negative is rejected by both.
- A phrase Rust creates restores to the same account in Rust, and its entropy re-encodes to the same phrase.
- Rust account types print no secret through `Debug`.

## Modified

### REQUIREMENT REQ-protocol-10

Rust SHALL verify every section and case of the TypeScript-generated conformance vectors `ts/conformance/vectors/00-07` and the shared `vectors/*.json` files it uses, reading them from byte-identical copies in `rs/vectors/` that ship in the crate. A missing vector file, a missing or unexpected section, or an unrecognized case SHALL fail the test. No Rust test SHALL return early or skip because a fixture is absent. A case Rust cannot yet check SHALL be named in one pending list with its issue; that list is empty. Vectors SHALL come from the TypeScript generators or the shared vector files, never from the Rust code under test. TypeScript qualification SHALL run `bun conformance/tools/verify.mjs`.

#### Acceptance Criteria

- `rs/tests/conformance.rs` passes in `rs/` and inside the extracted `.crate`; deleting a vector file, removing a section or renaming a negative case makes it fail.
- `rs/tests/cross_impl.rs` has no sibling-directory tests and no test in `rs/` returns on a missing path.
- `qualify.py ts` runs the TypeScript vector verifier and fails when it fails.
